# Article Draft

A portfolio benchmark should be treated as evidence, not decoration. The API validates provenance, workload identity, dependency and image digests, and comparability before accepting a number. Commands can quarantine or decide publication idempotently, while GraphQL consumers stay read-only.

The clean Node 24 Docker baseline reached 40.201 ms ingestion p95, 438.148 requests/second, and 24.119 ms GraphQL p95 with zero failures. The article links the V2 artifact and reproduction command, and limits comparison claims to matching comparability keys.
